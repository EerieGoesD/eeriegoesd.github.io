Guitar Hero Project - total notes for every Guitar Hero song

Guitar Hero Project lists the total number of notes in every song of Guitar Hero (PlayStation 2), Guitar Hero II (PlayStation 2 and Xbox 360) and Guitar Hero Encore: Rocks the 80s (PlayStation 2), on Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert. The table is sorted from the song with the fewest notes to the song with the most, and can be narrowed down by game, platform, difficulty and instrument. It covers the career songs, bonus tracks, secret songs and demo disc songs of each version. Songs played at 100% can be ticked off, and a bar shows how many are done. The note counts come from the song stats on ScoreHero.

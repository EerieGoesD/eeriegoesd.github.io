Guitar Hero Project - total notes for every Guitar Hero song

Guitar Hero Project lists the total number of notes in every song of Guitar Hero on PlayStation 2, on Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert. The table is sorted from the song with the fewest notes to the song with the most, and can be narrowed down by platform, difficulty and instrument. It covers the 30 career songs, the bonus tracks, the secret songs and the four songs from the Guitar Hero II demo disc, 53 songs in all. The note counts come from the song stats on ScoreHero.

Guitar Hero Project

Total note counts for every song in Guitar Hero on PlayStation 2, on every difficulty, sorted from fewest to most notes. Note counts from ScoreHero.

How to use it

  1. Tick the platforms, difficulties and instruments to show in the table.
  2. Tick a group's heading to show or hide the whole group.
  3. Read the table from the top: fewest notes first, most notes last.

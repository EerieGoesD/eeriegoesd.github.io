Deck Transfer Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Deck Transfer moves files between your Mac and your own Steam Deck. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

What we collect

Nothing. Deck Transfer has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not receive any of your files, your Deck's details or anything about how you use the app.

What stays on your Mac

The app stores your settings, the Decks you saved (including the SSH password you typed for each one), your saved folder paths, the folders you gave it access to, your transfer history, your scheduled syncs and a copy of your trial and purchase status in its own folder on your Mac. This data never leaves your Mac, and deleting the app deletes it.

Your files

Files you send or download travel directly between your Mac and your Steam Deck over your own cable or local network, encrypted by SSH. They do not pass through any server of ours or anyone else's. Downloads are saved only to the folder you choose.

Local network

To find your Deck, the app looks for devices on your local network and on a direct cable that accept SSH connections. It connects only to find your Deck and to sign in to the Deck you choose.

Purchases

The free trial and the one-time purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or your Apple ID.

Links

Links in the app, such as Report Issue, open in your web browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Children

Deck Transfer is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

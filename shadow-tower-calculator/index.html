Shadow Tower Calculator - item, enemy and drop reference with a damage calculator

Shadow Tower Calculator is a reference and damage calculator for Shadow Tower, the PlayStation game by FromSoftware. It lists all 303 items with their weight and fourteen attributes, and all 151 enemies with their HP, slash, smash, pierce and elemental defenses, the stats gained per kill and the chance of each item drop. The calculator finds the weapon and gear with the highest damage per second against a chosen enemy, using the damage formula, attack timing and weight limit from the game's code, and can limit the choice to the items in your inventory.

Drops: each enemy rolls once when it dies and gives at most one item. Gear you have already found never drops again, and the next item on that enemy gets its chance instead.

Your inventory and the HP, MP and SPD values are saved in this browser.

Item and enemy data for Shadow Tower on PlayStation, read from the game's own files, with a calculator that finds the weapon and gear that deal the most damage per second to a chosen enemy.

How to use it

  1. In the game, take off all your equipment and note your HP, MP and SPD. For HP and MP, use the second number (the maximum). Enter them below.
  2. In the Inventory tab, tick the weapons and gear you own.
  3. Choose an enemy. "Best gear you have" picks from your inventory, and "Best gear vs" picks from every item in the game.

Hold attack until the power gauge is full, then release. Bows: fire when the gauge is full. Gear always stays within your weight limit. Times use the game's 20 updates per second.
